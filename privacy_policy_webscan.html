1. Information Processing & Data Privacy

  • Web Scan Utility: The Web Scan feature functions purely as a secured web wrapper loading the official web client interfaces directly from official endpoints. All chat data, session cookies, and authentication keys remain strictly isolated within your device's standard local storage. We do not inspect, intercept, store, or transmit your messages, chats, or media to any external servers.
  • Direct Chat & Text Utilities: Phone numbers entered for direct messaging and custom text used for stylish fonts, captions, or text repeating are processed entirely on-device and are never logged or stored.
  • QR Studio: QR codes scanned or generated via the app are decoded and processed locally on your device in real-time.

2. Device Permissions Required

To provide core functionalities, the app requests the following optional system permissions:

  • Camera Permission: Required solely to scan QR codes for pairing devices or decoding QR data. Images from the camera are processed in memory and never uploaded.
  • Photo Library / Storage Access: Required only when you explicitly choose to save generated QR codes or downloaded media directly to your device's local gallery.
