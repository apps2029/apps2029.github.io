Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my account using Web Scan?

Open the app, navigate to Web Scan to display the QR code. On your other phone, open WhatsApp, go to Linked Devices, tap "Link a Device" and scan the code.

How does Direct Chat work?

Select the Country Code, enter the mobile number and your message, then tap Send. It opens a chat window directly without needing to save the contact number to your address book.

Why does my connection disconnect?

Make sure your primary phone remains connected to the internet. If you experience an unexpected logout, tap Reload from the top-right menu to refresh the session.
