App Support & Help Center

Welcome to the support page for the Self Attendance: Tracker App. We are here to help you get the most out of our application.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How do I add a new Subject or Job?

A: On the home screen, tap the "Add Subject or Job!" button at the bottom. Choose your type (Student or Work), enter the details, and tap Save.

Q: How is my data saved? Is it secure?

A: Yes! All your attendance data is 100% secure because it is saved entirely offline on your device. We do not use cloud servers to store your personal logs.

Q: How do I mark Duty Leave (DL) or Medical Leave (ML)?

A: In Student Mode, simply Long-Press on any date in the calendar. An advanced options menu will appear allowing you to select Duty Leave, Medical Leave, or Class Cancelled.

Q: How does the Salary Calculator work?

A: In Work Mode, tap the "Options / Info" button at the top right of the calendar screen and select "Calculate Your Salary". Enter your per-day base salary and overtime rate to get your exact earnings based on your present days, half days, and overtime hours.
