Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to College Attendance Tracker. We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application operates and explains our strict offline-first data approach.

Summary: College Attendance Tracker is an offline-first utility application. We do not require account creation, do not store your attendance records on external servers, and do not sell your personal information.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe students deserve complete control over their academic logs. Therefore:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, student ID, roll number, or phone number.
  • No Account Sign-up: You can use all core features without registering or logging in.
  • No Server Sync: Your subjects, lecture counts, bunk logs, and timetable entries remain entirely on your smartphone.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data created while using the application is stored securely on your local device storage using encrypted/isolated local databases (Hive):

  • Course and subject names
  • Attendance logs (Present, Absent, Duty Leave, Medical Leave, Cancelled)
  • Target attendance percentages (e.g., 75% criteria)
  • Custom lecture notes and time preferences

3. Backups and Data Ownership

Because your data is stored locally, uninstalling the app or resetting your phone will delete your data unless you create a backup.

  • Exporting Data: When you generate a backup file (JSON format), it is saved directly to your chosen local folder, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive account.
  • Importing Data: You have full control over importing backup files to restore your semester attendance records.

4. Device Permissions

To function properly, the app may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: Used strictly for optional local daily attendance reminders (e.g., 10:00 PM alert). No remote marketing push notifications are sent.
  • Storage / File Access: Used only when you choose to export or import your attendance backup files or PDF semester reports.

5. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

To keep the app free and maintain continuous updates, the app may display minimal advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks (such as Google AdMob). These networks may use non-identifying device advertising identifiers in compliance with standard mobile privacy guidelines.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is intended for general use by students and individuals seeking routine management.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published directly on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
