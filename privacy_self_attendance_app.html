Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

Thank you for choosing to use the Self Attendance: Tracker App. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Our application is designed with your privacy as our top priority. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on external servers.

All data you enter into the app—including attendance logs, shift details, salary calculations, and leave records—is stored strictly locally on your device.

2. Data Security

Because your data is stored locally on your mobile device, you are in complete control of it. We do not have access to your attendance records. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your records will be permanently deleted unless you have manually exported them.

3. Permissions

The app may request certain standard device permissions (such as Storage access) solely for the purpose of exporting your attendance reports to PDF format. We do not use these permissions to access your personal files or photos.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
