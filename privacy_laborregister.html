Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 15, 2026

LaborRegister is built with a privacy-first approach. We operate a 100% offline application. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal or business data on any external servers.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how LaborRegister ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information. Your privacy is paramount, and our core principle is simple: your business data belongs entirely to you and stays on your device.

2. Data Collection and Storage

LaborRegister operates locally on your device without requiring user registration or account creation.

  • Worker & Staff Records: Staff names, phone numbers, wage structures, and attendance statuses (Present, Absent, Half-day, Overtime) are stored strictly within local storage (Hive Database) on your device.
  • Financial & Cashbook Records: Advance payments, notes, Cash In, and Cash Out transactions remain on your local storage.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote databases or servers to collect, upload, or analyze your entries.

3. Device Permissions

The app may request access to certain system features solely to perform core functionality requested by you:

  • Storage / Files Access: Required only when you choose to export manual data backups (JSON files) or save/export generated PDF reports.
  • Sharing Services: Utilizes system-level sharing mechanisms (such as Apple AirDrop, WhatsApp, or Email) only when you explicitly tap the share action for PDF statements or backup archives.

4. Data Backup and Portability

Since LaborRegister operates offline, you have complete control over data backups and transfers:

  • You can create an encrypted/structured JSON backup file via the App Backup feature in Settings.
  • These files are saved wherever you decide (such as your personal iCloud Drive, Google Drive, or Local Files).
  • We have zero access to your backup files.

5. Third-Party Services

LaborRegister does not use third-party tracking tools, analytics SDKs, or targeted advertising frameworks. Your workflow is completely unmonitored.

6. Children’s Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since all data resides locally on the device, privacy protection is maintained unconditionally.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect any functional changes. Any modifications will be updated on this page along with an updated effective date.
