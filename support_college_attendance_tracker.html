Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q: How do I add a new Subject and set attendance targets?

A: On the Home screen, tap the "+ Add Subject!" button at the bottom. Enter your subject name and minimum attendance target (e.g., 75%), then tap Save.

Q: How does the "Safe Bunk" calculator work?

A: The app automatically calculates your percentage in real time. If you are above your target, it tells you exactly how many lectures you can safely skip. If you are below the target, it indicates how many consecutive classes you must attend to get back on track.

Q: How do I record Multiple Lectures, Labs, or Duty Leave (DL)?

A: In the Subject Calendar screen, simply Long-Press on any date. The Advanced Options modal lets you mark Duty Leave, Class Cancelled, add custom notes, or log multiple lectures (e.g., 1 Present + 1 Absent).

Q: Is my attendance data secure and private?

A: Yes, absolutely! All your academic records and calendar logs are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your personal attendance records on external cloud servers.

Q: How can I back up or transfer my records to a new phone?

A: Go to Settings → App Backup and tap "Export / Backup Data". Save the encrypted backup file (.json) to your Files or cloud storage. You can restore it anytime via the "Import / Restore Data" option.

Q: Can I export an attendance report for my college authorities?

A: Yes. Open any subject, tap the Share icon at the top right, and generate a clean, official PDF report of your monthly lecture records.
